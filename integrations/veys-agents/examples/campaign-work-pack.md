# Monsoon Roasters subscription campaign

## Proposed scope

Create one subscription page and two Instagram post drafts. The page will answer the delivery-frequency question and lead customers to the existing checkout. The owner supplies photos and approves copy. The ₹45,000 spending ceiling is the client's budget constraint. Fee, revision rounds and launch date remain open.

The proposal covers page content and the two post drafts. Checkout development, paid promotion and ongoing posting would require separate scope.

## Subscription page copy

**How often would you like fresh coffee?** Compare the available delivery schedules and choose the coffee plan that suits your routine.

**Primary action:** Compare delivery options.

For each plan, show its confirmed interval. Ask the owner for any pack-size and price details to display alongside it. After the comparison, use **Continue to checkout** and link to the existing checkout.

## Instagram drafts

**Post 1 · Delivery options.** Pair a product photo with a slide showing the confirmed intervals. “How often should fresh coffee arrive? Compare our subscription schedules and choose the one that suits your routine. See the delivery options on our subscription page.”

**Post 2 · Before checkout.** Use a second photo and an annotated preview of the page. “From the first bag to the next delivery, here’s where to find your plan details before checkout.” Schedule this once the page is available and the owner has approved both posts.

## Acceptance and next decision

The owner verifies the intervals and copy. The delivery lead checks that the intended checkout opens from mobile and desktop. The two post drafts use approved photos and point to the published page.
